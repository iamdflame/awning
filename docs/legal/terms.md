## 1. What Awning does

Awning turns a description of a business into a working storefront. From one sentence it drafts a name, copy and priced offers; it can open the business on a free `awning.sh` subdomain or, for a fee, with a custom domain, a phone number and an AI receptionist; and it lists the business in a public directory where people and AI agents can buy from it.

You use Awning through an AI agent connected to PayBox, a service run by MoonPay. PayBox's own terms apply to your PayBox account, wallet and approvals.

## 2. Payments are non-custodial

- Every sale is paid in USDC on Base **directly to the owner's wallet**. Awning never receives, holds or controls sales money.
- Awning receives only the Pro opening price, paid to Awning's wallet.
- Every payment is a request you approve in PayBox. Awning cannot charge you without your approval and never charges automatically.
- Payments on a blockchain are final. Awning cannot reverse a payment once it has settled. Check the amount and recipient PayBox shows you before you approve.
- You are responsible for the security of your wallet. Awning will never ask for a private key, a recovery phrase, a card number or a bank password.

## 3. Prices and what you get

- **Free:** a storefront at `<name>.awning.sh`, a directory listing and checkout paid to your wallet. $0.
- **Pro:** everything in Free, plus a custom domain for its first year and a phone number with an AI receptionist for the first month. The price is the domain's registrar price plus $9.00, shown before you approve. The quote is valid until the plan expires.
- Awning does not take a fee on sales.
- Renewal of the domain, phone number and receptionist after the included period is not yet available. Before anything included in Pro ends, we will tell you what it costs to keep it. If you do not renew, the phone line and receptionist may be switched off and the domain may expire. Nothing renews or charges automatically.

## 4. If an opening fails

- A Pro payment is settled only after the domain, phone number, receptionist and hosting have been set up. If setup fails before settlement, the payment is not taken.
- If a failure is discovered after the payment settled, Awning sends the same amount back to the wallet that paid.
- Awning does not refund sales. Sales go to the owner's wallet, and refunds for them are between the buyer and the owner.

## 8. Phone line and AI receptionist

- A Pro business gets a phone number answered by an AI voice agent, or by an automated spoken greeting that reads the business's details when no agent is attached.
- The receptionist answers from the business details and offers. It cannot take payments, change orders or make commitments for the owner beyond what those details say. It may take a message for the owner.
- Calls are carried by our telephony provider and, when the AI agent is attached, processed by our voice provider, which may record the audio and keep a transcript. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
- Owners must not use the line to call or message people who have not asked to be contacted.

## 9. Domains and phone numbers
